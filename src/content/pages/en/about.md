Hi, I'm Chinh (Hoàng Minh Chính). I build software in Hanoi, Vietnam.

I started in 2019 at a cloud communications platform and spent seven years there as a senior full-stack engineer: contact center software used by banks and insurers, the front end of an AI-assisted CRM, and the core modules of an internal ERP (accounting, contracts, payroll, sales KPIs) that people still use every day.

In 2026 I started [HC Labs](https://hclabs.co), a small software studio. We build operations software for Vietnamese businesses, and I am still the one who talks to the customer, designs the system and reads the code.

## What I write about here

I am learning to build a business in public. Expect the real work: decisions and the reasons behind them, things that went wrong, and what I learn from customers and other builders. Products show up as part of that story.

## What I work with

Go, TypeScript, React, Vue and PostgreSQL. Lately also a lot of AI coding agents, and the guardrails that keep them honest.
